About us

A community project, two years running

Maldivian Roblox started two years ago as a small passion project and has been in steady development ever since — new routes, new aircraft, and a community that keeps it flying.

We're a fan-made group and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the real-world Maldivian airline in any way. All liveries, names, and branding are used respectfully and all credit for the original designs belongs to their respective owners.

Disclaimer: This is an independent Roblox community project. We are not the real Maldivian airline and have no affiliation with them. Any resemblance to real liveries or branding is a tribute, not a claim of ownership.
